# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that address and applies in full to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people speak for it.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep the work about the work. We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this, and may ban people who keep doing so.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers privately at conduct@example.com (the maintainer replaces this address before the repository goes public). Reports are handled promptly and in confidence, and the people who report are not named without their consent. A report about a maintainer goes to another maintainer where there is one.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the harm and whether it repeats.
