Start here

loadout shares how your team works with the AI tools you already use: Claude Code and GitHub Copilot. Your team writes it down once, as small helpers. loadout copies them onto your machine and keeps them up to date. You ask your AI tool as usual, and it uses your team's helpers.

loadout never runs AI and never sees your questions. Your own AI tool does that, on your machine.

How it works

  1. A team publishes helpers

    Someone on your team writes down how you work and publishes it to this registry.

  2. loadout installs them

    Claude CodeGitHub Copilot

    The loadout command copies them into your AI tool and brings new versions.

  3. You ask your AI tool

    Ask as you always do. Your AI tool now knows how your team works.

Words you will meet

The two you meet most: you choose an agent, and a skill chooses itself.

Agent

A helper with one job, such as planning a change. You pick it by name in your AI tool.

Like a colleague you call over.

Skill

A how-to that your AI tool reads on its own when your question matches it.

Like a recipe in the drawer.

Knowledge base

A folder of documents your AI tool can read when it needs them, such as runbooks or team rules.

Like the team binder.

Package

One agent, skill or knowledge base, with a version number. A new version replaces the old one on your machine.

Registry

This website. Teams publish their packages here, and loadout installs them from here.

Project, team, layer

Whose package it is. A project holds what all its teams share (layer L0). A team holds its own (layer L1).
